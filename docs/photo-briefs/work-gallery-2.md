## What is in frame — the holes, and what came out of them

**Two or more finished core holes** in a concrete wall or slab: perfectly circular, edges sharp, the
inner wall smooth enough to see it is smooth, no spalling at the rim, no cracking between them.
Directly below or beside them, **the extracted cores standing on end on the floor**, cut faces towards
the camera, reinforcement bar ends visible in the cross-section.

That pairing is the argument in one picture: the diameter that was asked for went in, and a whole
cylinder came out. A single hole photographed alone reads as damage; holes with their cores beside
them read as method.

Everything real stays in: the marked-out crosses beside the holes, the slurry ring, the sheeting on
the floor, the sleeve already pushed into one hole if the plumber got there first.

## Where the camera stands

**About 1.2 metres back, square to the wall, landscape, phone level.** Get the holes and the cores in
one frame with the cores in the lower third. If the holes are in a floor rather than a wall, crouch —
shoot from about knee height across the slab rather than straight down, so the holes stay round-ish
and keep their depth instead of flattening to grey discs.

Second frame, closer: one hole filling most of the picture with its core standing against the wall
beside it. Send both; the close one is usually the keeper.

Light: side light. Have someone hold the phone light **off to one side and slightly above** so each
rim throws a small shadow into its hole. Light from the camera position flattens everything.

## What must survive the cover crop

- **Holes and cores together in the middle band.** If the cores are at the bottom edge they will be
  cropped off and the picture loses half its meaning.
- 10% clear margin on all four sides; the cover crop takes the edges and nothing here rescues a
  subject framed against one.
- At 320px what reads is **circles and cylinders**. Make them big in the frame. A wall photographed
  from four metres away with three small holes in it reads as a blank wall.
- Landscape only.

## What must stay out of frame

- **Any face other than אור שוורץ's** (business-facts §A).
- Licence plates, apartment numbers, nameplates, buzzer panels, letterboxes, an identifiable view
  through a window, delivery notes, invoices, a plan with the client's name in the title block.
- Any certificate, licence, insurance paper, badge, rating or review.
- Personal belongings in an occupied home; an office with readable screens, whiteboards or documents.
- A before/after pair unless both frames are your own, of the same job, from the same position, with
  the client's agreement.
- Unsafe practice: an unanchored rig, a large core barrel held by hand, no eye protection.

## How to send it

Original file, largest size, **no crop, no filter, no blur effect**. With it: **element and thickness,
wet or dry, how many holes and at what diameter**, and whether the client agreed to publication. If it
was a prestressed slab, tell us — that one may be better left unpublished.
